# 📊 Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 🚀 Project Overview

The **Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It analyzes retail sales performance, product categories, profitability, customer purchasing patterns, and yearly sales trends.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful visual insights, helping businesses understand their sales performance, identify top-performing products, and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify the highest-performing product categories.
- Track quantity ordered across different years.
- Compare sales performance across product lines.
- Identify top-selling products.
- Analyze trends and factors influencing sales.
- Build interactive dashboards for business decision-making.
## 📌 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Performance Dashboard
- Total Profit KPI
- Total Sales KPI
- Most Ordered Product
- Sales comparison across different years
- Sales distribution by product line
- Product-wise sales analysis
- Year-wise quantity ordered

### 2. Product & Sales Analysis
- Sales by Product Line
- Quantity Ordered by Product Line
- Top Products by Sales
- Top Products by Quantity Ordered
- Order Day Analysis
- Sales performance trends

### 3. Key Influencers Analysis
- Identifies factors influencing sales.
- Analyzes product descriptions and product categories.
- Highlights segments associated with higher sales.
- Uses Power BI Key Influencers visual for deeper analysis.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Overall revenue generated |
| Total Profit | Overall business profit |
| Quantity Ordered | Total products ordered |
| Most Ordered Product | Product with the highest order quantity |
| Sales Growth | Comparison of sales across selected periods |

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Classic Cars contribute the highest sales among the product lines.
- Vintage Cars represent another significant sales category.
- The 1992 Ferrari 360 Spider Red is among the top-performing products by sales.
- Product categories show different purchasing and sales patterns.
- Year-wise quantity analysis helps understand ordering trends.
- Key Influencers analysis helps identify factors associated with sales performance.
